About Flight Lab

Learn → Experiment → Fly → Fail Safely → Understand → Master

Flight Lab is an independent, open-access educational project designed to turn difficult aviation concepts into interactive learning experiences. The v0.1 shell works locally; its four current playable labs are hosted externally and therefore require an internet connection to launch.

Training disclaimer: Flight Lab is an educational supplement only. It is not approved flight training material, an operational flight-planning tool, a substitute for certified instruction, aircraft documentation, or official regulations. It is not affiliated with Oxford, ICAO, EASA, FAA, any airline, aircraft manufacturer, or training organization.
